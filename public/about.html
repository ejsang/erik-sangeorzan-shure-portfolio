I’m a Software Engineer at Ford with a University of Michigan B.S.E. in Computer Engineering and a Minor in Music. My professional path connects automotive acoustics, embedded Android/audio bring-up, and C++ diagnostic systems.

Engineering background

I graduated magna cum laude in December 2021 and joined Ford in March 2022. The Ford College Graduate program included rotations in Core NetCom Diagnostics, IVI Audio, ADAS Localization/Odometry, and ADAS Embedded Data Operations. I returned to Core NetCom after the program.

My hands-on professional Android/AOSP work came during a September 2022–March 2023 IVI Audio rotation, alongside my broader C++ systems work at Ford. The current systems work centers on C++ engineering tools, communications libraries, testing, integration, and maintainability.

Ford’s Clean Code Green, Orange, Yellow and White Belts are internal certifications that complement my practical TDD, code-quality, and mentoring work.

Leadership & service

Ann Arbor Jaycees

I bring people together for community projects and group service through the Ann Arbor Jaycees. I joined in December 2023, became a Director in January 2025, and have served as Community Vice President since January 2026.

I help plan and run Ann Arbor’s Fourth of July Parade and coordinate group volunteering at NAMIWalks and the Girls on the Run 5K. Other projects include organizing clothes at the Michigan Foster Care Closet, volunteering at Food Gatherers, helping beautify Avalon Housing properties, cleaning up Ann Arbor’s County Farm Park, and making blankets for cats and dogs at the local humane society.

Through Bountiful Baskets, our chapter provided 100 boxes of Thanksgiving food to families in need. Recognition includes Director of the Year and Chairperson of the Year, plus Project of the Year for Bountiful Baskets.
