The manual workflow

Amplifier electrical validation required roughly five to eight hours of manual effort. Repeated operator work made automation a useful engineering problem alongside the acoustic testing itself.

I built a C# tool to automate the electrical-validation workflow. The team adopted it for production testing, reducing the required human effort to less than one hour.

Measurement and listening

This experience gave me a professional connection to audible behavior and physical audio quality alongside software automation. My role was acoustic validation and tooling.
